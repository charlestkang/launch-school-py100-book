EXERCISE 3
Identify at least 2 differences between lists and tuples. Identify at least 2 ways that lists and 
tuples are similar.


ANSWER:
Differences: Lists, unlike tuples, are mutable. Lists are not hashable but tuples are (if every 
element in the tuple is hashable). 
Additionally, lists use square brackets where tuples use parentheses (really tuples use commas,
not parentheses, but the parentheses are idiomatic).

Similarities: Lists and tuples are (1) collections (2) sequences (3) iterable (which follows 
from (2)), (4) ordered and (5) heterogenous (meaning they may contain more than one kind of object)
